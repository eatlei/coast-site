我的数据存在哪里？Where is my data stored?+

只在你的设备和你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库里。Coast 没有服务器，开发者看不到你的任何数据。Only on your device and in your own iCloud private database. Coast has no servers; the developer cannot see any of your data.

如何在多台设备间同步？How do I sync across devices?+

登录同一个 Apple 账户并开启 iCloud，数据会自动同步。Sign in with the same Apple account with iCloud enabled, and your data syncs automatically.

能导入以前的账单吗？Can I import my old records?+

可以。支持 CSV / XLSX，UTF-8 和 GB18030 编码自动识别，iCost 导出的文件开箱即用。账户名会模糊匹配，自动去重，整批可撤销。Yes. CSV / XLSX with automatic UTF-8 and GB18030 detection; iCost exports work out of the box. Account names are fuzzy-matched, duplicates skipped, and the whole batch can be undone.

免费版能用多久？How long does the free version last?+

永久。记账不限量，账户、预算、导入导出和 FI 倒计时都不收费。会员解锁的是达成区间、场景模拟这类「推演未来」的功能。Forever. Unlimited entries, accounts, budgets, import/export and the FI countdown are all free. Pro unlocks projection features like achievement range and scenarios.
