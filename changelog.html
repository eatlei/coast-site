Coast 更新日志

每一次更新，都离自由更近一步。

1.02026 年 8 月

首个正式版本。Coast 是一款以 FIRE（财务自由）进度为核心的记账 App——记账只是输入手段，真正回答的问题是「我离财务自由还有多远」。

记账

快速记账

从任意页面一键唤起，金额 → 分类 → 完成，三步搞定。支持分期、手续费、AA 分摊、借入借出。

周期账单

自动发现重复消费模式，一次设置、每月自动入账。再也不用手动记房租、订阅费。

CSV 导入

支持从支付宝、微信等导出的 CSV 一键导入，自动匹配分类、去重、识别转账。历史账单冷启动不再是问题。

分期中心

所有进行中的分期一目了然：已还多少、还剩多少、每期金额与手续费。

数据

月度数据面板

收支趋势、分类环形图、预算执行率、储蓄率——一屏看清这个月的财务全貌。

预算

按分类设预算，实时追踪剩余额度。超支时在账单页直接标红提醒。

日历视图

日历上每一天标记有无账单，点击查看当日明细，月度节奏一目了然。

资产

资产快照

每月记录各账户余额，自动生成净资产曲线。支持多币种、自动汇率折算。

资产类型

现金及等价物、投资（股票、基金、理财）、负债（信用卡、贷款），按类别汇总。

FIRE 进度

FI 仪表盘

实时计算 Lean FI、标准 FI 数字与进度百分比，追踪 Coast FIRE、Barista FIRE 状态。

储蓄率追踪

当月储蓄率 + 12 月移动平均，FIRE 践行者最关心的第一指标。

被动收入覆盖率

被动收入占必要支出的比例——100% 就是你不用上班的那一天。

更多

Coast Changelog

Every update, one step closer to freedom.

1.0August 2026

The first release. Coast is a personal finance app built around FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) — tracking spending is just the input; the real question it answers is "how far am I from financial freedom?"

Transactions

Quick Add

One-tap entry from any screen. Amount, category, done — three steps. Supports installments, fees, split bills, and IOUs.

Recurring Transactions

Automatically detects spending patterns. Set it once, and rent, subscriptions, and bills are logged each month.

CSV Import

Import from Alipay, WeChat Pay, and other CSV exports. Auto-categorizes, deduplicates, and detects transfers. Cold-start your history in minutes.

Installment Center

All active installments at a glance: how much paid, how much left, per-period amount and fees.

Insights

Monthly Dashboard

Income and expense trends, category donut chart, budget progress, savings rate — your full financial picture on one screen.

Budgets

Set budgets by category, track remaining balance in real time. Over-budget items are flagged directly on the transaction list.

Calendar View

Each day on the calendar is marked with transactions. Tap to see the daily breakdown and find your spending rhythm.

Assets

Asset Snapshots

Record account balances each month to build a net worth curve over time. Multi-currency with automatic exchange rate conversion.

Asset Types

Cash and equivalents, investments (stocks, funds, savings), liabilities (credit cards, loans) — aggregated by type.

FIRE Progress

FI Dashboard

Real-time Lean FI and Standard FI numbers with progress percentage. Tracks your Coast FIRE and Barista FIRE status.

Savings Rate

Current month savings rate plus 12-month moving average — the single most important metric for FIRE practitioners.

Passive Income Coverage

The ratio of passive income to essential expenses — when it hits 100%, that's the day you no longer need a paycheck.

And More

  • iCloud Sync — seamless multi-device, stored only in your personal iCloud private database
  • Backup & Restore — local JSON backups, export and import anytime
  • Dark Mode — follows system setting or manual toggle
  • Themes — multiple color schemes to choose from
  • Bilingual — switch between Chinese and English in-app
  • Category Management — custom categories, icons, sorting, with subcategories
  • Frequent Purchases — one-tap reuse for recurring buys
  • Privacy First — no accounts, no servers, no tracking. Your data belongs to you
